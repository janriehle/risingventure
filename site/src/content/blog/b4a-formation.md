We are glad to announce Rising Venture's transition from the search to the operating phase in August 2017. We closed a three-month due-diligence and financing period with the acquisition of two companies: **Glambox**, the biggest female subscription service in Latin America, and **Men's Market**, the biggest male-focused beauty e-commerce in the region.

Rising Venture, together with investors, acquired and consolidated the companies in July 2017 and is now operating a holding company that was founded for the purpose of consolidation and synergy creation: **B4A**.
